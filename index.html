Every account has a code and belongs to a category (e.g. 310-0000 Cash & Bank → 310-C001 Cash). Add, re-code, rename or remove accounts and categories. System accounts and accounts that already have transactions can be re-coded or renamed (non-system only) but not deleted.

Opening Balance Maintenance

Enter the balance of each account on the day your books start. The difference is posted automatically to Opening Balance Equity (100-0002), so the books stay balanced. Receivables and Customer Deposits come from invoices / wallet top-ups and are not entered here.
